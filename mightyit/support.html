Common questions

Where is my game data stored?
Your run history and card statistics are stored only on your device.

How do I reset my progress?
Open Settings in Mighty IT, select Reset Run History, and confirm the reset.

The app is not working as expected. What should I try?

  1. Close and reopen Mighty IT.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Install the latest Mighty IT update from the App Store.
  4. If the problem continues, contact us using the email address above.
